Skvotiškės, near Aukštadvaris

An hour outside Vilnius in the Trakai district. Come by car, by bus and shuttle, or walk in through fields & forest.

The site

Skvotiškės, near Aukštadvaris, Trakai district.

Coordinates 54.572083, 24.483722

Open in Google Maps →

Arrive by car

Use the Google Maps link or the coordinates to find the festival location. One hour by car from Vilnius or from Kaunas. Parking is available on site.

Carpooling

Ticket holders receive an email with an invitation to a Telegram group, where carpooling gets arranged.

Arrive by bus

Bus tickets: autobusubilietai.lt

Bus stop “Aukštadvaris, LT, Trakų rajonas”.

The festival site is around an hour's walk from Aukštadvaris along a beautiful field & forest road.

Shuttle pickups from the Aukštadvaris bus stop — info in the following section.

Shuttle pickups

Free pickups run between the Aukštadvaris bus stop and the festival. Please inform us on your arrival time in the form below and secure a place.

What to bring, what's on site

Short version: a tent, a warm layer, a bowl, a bank card and a USB stick.

Safety

  • Please be responsible for yourself and for others, and let's create a safer space for everybody.
  • A first aid kit will be available at the bar.
  • Please don't bring any glass to the festival.
  • Please don't start fires — one central bonfire will be maintained.
  • No swimming at night.
  • Please bring water for yourself.

Camping

Camping grounds will be available (no indoor accommodation) — bring your tent and a sleeping bag. Pack something warm too, the nights get breezy.

Water

Please bring water for yourself — we recommend 3 L per day.

Sadly the well water is not available for drinking this year, but we'll have some fresh water on site.

Food

A food truck with vegan and omnivore options at fair prices, cash or card. Bring a bowl and cutlery from home to avoid waste.

A grill and one designated campfire are there if you'd rather cook yourself. Please don't start any other fires — let's keep the area safe and clean.

Bar

Beer, cocktails and non-alcoholic drinks — kombucha, Lynxmonadas, coffee. Prices are friendly and every drink comes with a chat, so please use the bar instead of bringing your own.

We use rental cups to cut waste. Your own favourite cup is very welcome.

Please don't bring any glass to the festival.

Paying

Card and phone payments everywhere, and some cash accepted.

Showers

Available for a small fee — or take a dip in the pond.

Toilets

Pee in the bushes, poop in the tidy compost toilets.

Open decks

Bring your USB, there's time set aside for you. Check the lineup for the exact slots.

Workshops

Workshops run through the weekend. We'll announce them soon.

Sunday cake

Join us at 1 PM on Sunday for PSR's 9th birthday cake. Woohoo!
